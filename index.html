About Us | எங்களைப் பற்றி

Geo Institute of Health Care was established in 2025 at Virudhunagar, near New Bus Stand.

Our main motive is to provide quality healthcare education and practical learning opportunities, especially for students from financially and educationally challenging backgrounds.

We aim to help students build their knowledge, practical skills and confidence for a career in the healthcare sector.

More than 100+ students have benefited through our institute. Our students are gaining practical training opportunities in hospitals in Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, Trichy and Virudhunagar.

Admissions are open. Students interested in Nursing and healthcare-related courses can contact us for course and admission details.
